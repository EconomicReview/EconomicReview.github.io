What happens to your paper

Research submissions are anonymized before anyone reads them; only the Editor-in-Chief can connect a manuscript to its author. Two referees read each paper independently and the board decides on their recommendation. Perspectives go straight to the editors.

Written feedback, every time

Every submission — accepted or not — receives written feedback from its readers. You will never get a bare no.
