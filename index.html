What happens to your paper

Research submissions are anonymized before anyone reads them; only the Editor-in-Chief can match a manuscript to its author. Two referees read each paper on their own, and the board decides with their recommendations in hand. Perspectives go straight to the editors.

Feedback on every submission

Every submission gets written comments back, including the ones we turn down. You will never get a bare no.
