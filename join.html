Reviewing

Referees are the journal. As a reviewer you read at most two papers per cycle, and a careful read with written comments takes roughly 2–3 hours per paper — that is the whole commitment. You’ll see how arguments hold up under scrutiny and what separates a good term paper from a publishable one, which tends to make your own writing better. Reviewer training runs in late September; no prior experience is expected.

You can volunteer by emailing the board, through TribeLink, or by checking the reviewer box when you submit your own work — authors often make the best referees.

Editing and staff

Editors run the two tracks: shepherding research through review, working with Perspectives authors on drafts, and putting the issue together. Staff also handle production, outreach, and events with the Economics Club. The current board serves through officer elections in April — staff who have worked on the Review during the year are best placed to stand.
