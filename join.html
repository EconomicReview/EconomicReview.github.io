Reviewing

As a reviewer you read at most two papers per cycle, and a careful read with written comments takes roughly 2–3 hours per paper. That is the full time commitment. Reviewer training runs in late September; no prior experience is expected. Reading other people’s arguments closely also happens to be good practice for your own writing.

You can volunteer by emailing the board, through TribeLink, or by checking the reviewer box on the form when you submit your own work.

Editing and staff

Editors run the two tracks: they move research papers through anonymized review and work with Perspectives authors on drafts. Staff handle production, outreach, and events with the Economics Club, and the whole board puts the spring issue together. The current board serves through officer elections in April, and staff who have worked on the Review during the year are best placed to stand.

First steps

Come to the interest meeting in September, or just email us. There is no application to read a paper.
