What we publish

The Review publishes two kinds of work by W&M students. Research is the journal’s core: full-length empirical or theoretical papers, selected by double-blind review against semester deadlines. Perspectives are 600–1,200 word op-eds and policy briefs that the editors review on a rolling basis. Most of what we publish is economics, but public policy, finance, economic history, and data science applied to economic questions are all in scope.

The editorial process

Every research submission is anonymized before anyone reads it. Only the Editor-in-Chief can match a manuscript to its author; referees never see names, and authors aren’t told who read their paper. Two referees read each submission independently (no reviewer handles more than two papers in a cycle), and the editorial board makes the final decision on their recommendation.

Perspectives don’t go through refereeing. The editors read each piece and respond directly, usually within a few weeks.

Authors receive written feedback with every decision, including rejections.

Where published work lives

Accepted work is published on this site. For research, the version of record will also be deposited in W&M ScholarWorks, the university library’s permanent open-access repository, the same system that keeps the James Blair Historical Review available long after its student editors graduate.
