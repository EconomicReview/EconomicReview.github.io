Perspectives are open

The Review reads Perspectives on a rolling basis. There is no deadline, and pieces don’t wait on refereeing: the editors read each one, reply with written feedback, and publish what they accept here, usually within a few weeks. Send an argument you can back with evidence, on whatever economic question you’ve been chewing on.
